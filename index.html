Common questions

Why does Reclaim ask for Screen Time permission?

Reclaim uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to protect the apps you choose. Screen Time authorization is required for these core features.

Does Reclaim see what I do inside other apps?

No. Reclaim does not read the content of the apps you protect and does not monitor what you type, view, or do inside those apps.

How does temporary access work?

When protection is active, you can reclaim access to selected protected apps for 5 or 10 minutes. When that period ends, Reclaim restores protection automatically.

What should I include in a bug report?

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Whether the issue involved a recurring schedule, Protect Now, reclaiming access, or automatic re-protection.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.
